Signal System Lab

A virtual laboratory for Signals and Systems

Introduction

Signals are the language of electronics, communication and biomedical systems. A heartbeat, a radio wave and a sensor reading are all signals. Signal Bench lets you create two continuous-time signals, combine them with standard operations, and watch the result update instantly, so the theory from class appears on the graph.

Objectives
  • Visualize basic and real-world signals, and see how amplitude, frequency and phase shape them.
  • Apply time shifting, time scaling and time reversal to a signal.
  • Perform addition, subtraction, multiplication, convolution and cross-correlation on two signals.
  • Measure the energy, power, RMS value and mean of a signal.
How to use
1. Set the signalsStart from a quick experiment, or choose the type and parameters of Signal A and Signal B.
2. Choose an operationSelect add, subtract, multiply, convolve or correlate.
3. Read the resultStudy the result graph and the measurements below it. Touch or hover on any graph for exact values.
